Gentle Female Beagle Names

She'll follow a scent to the ends of the earth, but she'd rather be curled against your leg on a quiet afternoon. The Beagle is one of the most tender-hearted dogs there is — curious without being reckless, affectionate without being clingy, and gentle in a way that feels completely genuine. These names come from meadows, old stories, and the soft parts of language that suit a dog who makes the world feel a little kinder just by being in it.

The Names

Blossom

Old English — The gentle opening of a flower; beauty that arrives in its own time without any rush

Nell
