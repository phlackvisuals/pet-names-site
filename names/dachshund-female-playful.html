Playful Female Dachshund Names

She is six inches tall and absolutely certain she runs the household. The Dachshund was bred to hunt badgers underground — which means she has the courage of a dog five times her size, the stubbornness of a mule, and a sense of humour that is entirely her own. These names celebrate her low-to-the-ground greatness: drawn from German roots, spirited heroines, and the kind of playful energy that makes even a short walk feel like an adventure.
