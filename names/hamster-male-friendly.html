Friendly Male Hamster Names

He climbs into your hand like he owns it, stuffs his cheeks with breathtaking efficiency, and spins his wheel with the cheerful dedication of someone who genuinely loves what he does. A friendly hamster is one of life's small but genuine pleasures — warm, round, surprisingly personable, and always pleased to see you if you arrive bearing sunflower seeds. These names are drawn from the cosy, the sweet, the rotund, and the warmhearted, sized just right for the world's most enthusiastic tiny companion.
