Mysterious Female Black Cat Names

She moves without sound, watches without blinking, and knows things she isn't telling. A black cat carries centuries of myth on her sleek shoulders — familiar to witches, sacred to goddesses, and sovereign of every shadow she chooses to inhabit. These names come from the dark corners of folklore, ancient pantheons, and the language of the night sky.
