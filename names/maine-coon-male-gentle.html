Gentle Male Maine Coon Names

He is enormous, and he knows it, and he uses that size exclusively for good. The Maine Coon is the gentle giant of the cat world — one of the largest domestic breeds on earth, yet possessed of a temperament so mild and affectionate that he gets along with everyone he meets. He chirps rather than meows, follows you from room to room like a devoted shadow, and drapes himself across your lap with the unhurried confidence of a cat who has never needed to prove anything. These names are drawn from the forests of New England, Norse legend, the great wilderness, and the language of warmth and quiet, unassuming strength.

The Names
