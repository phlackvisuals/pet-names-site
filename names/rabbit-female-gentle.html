Gentle Female Rabbit Names

She approaches the world with the lightest of steps — ears soft, eyes warm, content to sit beside you and simply be. A gentle rabbit is a rare kind of quiet joy: she doesn't demand attention, she earns it, one slow blink and one velvet nudge at a time. These names come from meadows, old stories, and the tender corners of language where softness is treated as the strength it truly is.

The Names
